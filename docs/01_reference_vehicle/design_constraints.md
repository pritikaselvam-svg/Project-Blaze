# Design Constraints

## Objective

Compare alternative electrical powertrain architectures while holding the major vehicle-level conditions constant.

## Fixed Conditions

The following remain constant across architectures:

- Vehicle platform
- Wheelbase
- Front track
- Rear track
- Overall vehicle envelope
- Vehicle mass target
- Driver mass
- Total peak tractive power
- Battery chemistry
- Total battery energy
- System voltage range
- Tyre specification
- Cooling assumptions
- Driver input
- Driving cycle
- Baseline operating conditions

## Architecture Variables

The following may change between architectures:

- Motor count
- Motor placement
- Motor power distribution
- Battery physical segmentation
- Inverter placement
- High-voltage cable routing
- Resulting mass distribution
- Electrical topology

## Experimental Principle

Only the defined architectural variables should be changed when comparing the candidate architectures.

This is intended to isolate the effects of electrical powertrain architecture on:

- Electrical efficiency
- Voltage drop
- Electrical losses
- Thermal behaviour
- Vehicle mass distribution
- Centre of gravity
- Yaw moment of inertia
- Dynamic performance
- Energy consumption
- System complexity
