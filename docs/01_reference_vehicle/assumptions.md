# Modelling Assumptions

This document distinguishes engineering assumptions from values derived from external references or Formula Student regulations.

## Vehicle Geometry

A representative wheelbase, track width and overall vehicle envelope are selected within the Formula Student Electric design space.

## Mass

An unladen vehicle mass of 240 kg is used as a modelling assumption.

A 68 kg driver is assumed, giving a total reference mass of 308 kg.

## Battery

An NMC lithium-ion battery with 7.5 kWh usable/reference energy is used as the initial modelling assumption.

## Voltage

The study considers a 400–600 V system-voltage range.

An exact nominal voltage will be selected during the architecture design stage based on the selected powertrain components and experimental requirements.

## Power

The total peak tractive-system power is constrained to 80 kW for the architecture comparison.

## Cooling

A common liquid-cooling capability is assumed for all architectures so that cooling capacity does not become an uncontrolled variable.

## Operating Conditions

The baseline comparison assumes dry-road conditions and identical driver inputs/driving cycles across architectures.

## Important Note

Assumptions may be revised if later analysis shows that a different value is required for physical consistency or experimental feasibility.

Any revision must be documented with its rationale.
